---
title: "When a licence ends"
description: "What happens to a workspace, its people and its devices after a licence ends: the notice, the read-only period, the licence-ended page, exporting your data, and when it is deleted."
---

A licence that ends does not lock a workspace in one step. The workspace moves
through four phases, and each one has a clear date. You keep your data readable
and exportable for most of that time.

<Note>
  Once Blindsight switches the licence lifecycle on for your platform, these steps
  apply. Until then, a workspace behaves as before.
</Note>

## The phases

The dates count from the day the licence ended.

| Phase | When | What works |
| ----- | ---- | ---------- |
| **Notice** | The first 7 days. | Everything. A banner says the licence ended and when the workspace becomes read-only. |
| **Read-only** | The next 30 days. | You can read and export. Changes are refused. Protection on your devices stops. |
| **Ended** | From day 37. | Sign-in, the licence-ended page and export only. |
| **Export closes** | Day 60. | Export stops. Sign-in and the licence-ended page still open. |
| **Deletion** | Day 90, about 3 months. | Your data is due for deletion. |

When a Blindsight administrator revokes or suspends a licence on purpose, the
notice is 5 days instead of 7. The read-only and later dates move with it, except
that export always closes 60 days after the end and deletion falls due 90 days
after it.

Renewing the licence puts the workspace back to normal. Devices that stopped
protecting come back by themselves.

## The notice

During the notice the whole console works. A banner at the top of the page says:

- **Your licence has expired**, when it ran out on its date. It names that date.
- **Your licence has ended**, when it was taken away. It says whether the licence
  was suspended, removed or withdrawn.

The banner also names the date the workspace becomes read-only, and tells you to
contact your Blindsight representative to renew.

Administrators of the workspace also get an email when each phase begins:
the notice, the read-only period, the closing of the workspace, and a last
warning in the 14 days before deletion. Each email goes out once per phase.

## Read-only

The banner reads **This workspace is read-only**. It says:

- Changes are blocked and protection on your devices has stopped. (Agent 1.7.0 and later. An older agent keeps its settings until it is uninstalled with a code.)
- Until when you can read your data.
- Until when you can export it.

In this phase an API key or app token that tries to change something gets
`403 LICENSE_READ_ONLY`.

A few changes stay open, because they reduce exposure. You can still:

- delete an API key, an app token or an integration token;
- remove a person, or end an MSP's access to the workspace;
- revoke or delete a device and revoke an enrollment token;
- revoke a SCIM token;
- get an [uninstall code](/endpoint/uninstall#uninstall-codes) or turn the
  uninstall requirement off.

## The licence-ended page

From day 37 every page of the console opens the **Your licence has ended** page.
It is the only page, apart from sign-in and the links people arrive through
(password reset, invitations, email change and unsubscribe).

The page offers:

- **Export data**, while the export window is open. The page says, for example,
  "You can export your data until 30 December 2026. To use Blindsight again, renew
  or contact us." After day 60 it says "The export window has closed. To use
  Blindsight again, renew or contact us." and the button is gone.
- **Renew or contact us**.
- **Get an uninstall code**, for an administrator. See below.
- **Leave workspace**, for an MSP member working inside a client workspace.
- **Log out**.

## Export your data

**Export data** downloads the audit trail as a CSV file. It is open for 60 days
after the licence ended, in the read-only phase and after it. After that date the
export is refused too.

## What happens to the devices

Agent 1.7.0 and later. An older agent keeps its settings until it is uninstalled with a code.

When the read-only phase begins, the workspace tells each enrolled agent that the
licence ended. An agent acts only on that exact answer, and only after it has
seen it twice, at least a minute apart. A network error, a timeout or an outage
at the platform never counts.

The agent then hands the machine back:

- It removes its proxy settings and the Blindsight root certificate it installed.
- It stops inspecting AI traffic.
- It releases the uninstall requirement, so the agent can be removed without a
  code.
- It shows the person a notice: "Your organisation's Blindsight licence ended".

A Mac agent is handed back the same way. If the licence is renewed, the agent
comes back by itself. A Mac makes a new root certificate and waits for its
approval before it inspects again.

### Uninstall code from the ended page

Agent 1.7.0 and later. An older agent keeps its settings until it is uninstalled with a code. An older agent may still ask for a code before it lets itself be removed. An
administrator can issue one from the licence-ended page:

1. Select **Get an uninstall code**.
2. Copy the code. It works on every device for 72 hours.
3. Enter it where the agent asks to be removed.

If the code cannot be issued, the page says "The code could not be issued. Try
again."

## When the platform goes silent

Separately from a licence ending, an agent can step aside when the platform has
not answered for a long time. After 14 days without any answer, plus awake time
and failed attempts to reach the platform, the agent hands the machine back
in the same way. It returns by itself when the platform answers again. This
removes nothing.

Agent 1.7.0 and later. An older agent keeps its settings until it is uninstalled with a code. This is off by default in agent 1.7.0. An administrator of the device turns it on
with the managed configuration value `SilenceRelease`, and changes the number of
days with `SilenceDays`. On Windows these live under `HKLM\SOFTWARE\Blindsight`.
On a Mac they are the `dev.blindsight` managed preferences.

## Deletion

The workspace shows the date the data is due for deletion. The platform sends the
last warning email in the 14 days before it.
