---
title: "Device linking and person records"
description: "How a laptop is linked to the person who uses it, so a policy assigned to that person reaches their device."
---

A policy assigned to a person reaches that person's laptop once the laptop is
linked to them. Workspaces that use [Intune](/endpoint/connect-intune) get these
links made for them.

## How a laptop is linked

- **An exact match links automatically.** When the Intune user of a laptop matches
  a person's email address, the laptop is linked to that person. The match ignores
  letter case.
- **The link follows the primary user.** When the Intune primary user changes, the
  link moves to the new person.
- **Everything else is a suggestion.** When there is no exact match, or the link
  needs a decision, a suggested link appears for an admin to confirm or dismiss.
- **People with a policy get a suggestion.** A person who has a policy assigned, by
  name or through a group, gets a suggested link instead of an automatic one. The
  suggestion lists every policy that starts to apply once you confirm it.
- **Platform owners always get a suggestion.**
- **Workspaces that do not name people** never link or suggest. See
  [Who is named in the console](/endpoint/who-is-named).

## Review and undo links

Suggested links are listed in **Pending actions** and on **Endpoint → Devices**.
Confirm or dismiss each one.

Every link, change and removal is recorded in the
[audit trail](/compliance/audit-trail). You can undo a recent link from **Recent
links**. A dismissed suggestion is not offered again, and an undone link is not
made again.

## What a link gives the person

A link made from Intune selects the person's policy. It does not give that person
any authority on the device, such as pausing protection, overriding rules or adding
device rules. Those come only after an admin confirms the link or the person signs
in. See [Identity](/endpoint/identity).

## Person records

An Intune user with no console account becomes a person record, so you can assign
policies to them.

- A person record has no password, no sign-in and no role.
- You can assign it policies and link it to a laptop.
- It does not count toward your seat limit and does not appear in the **Users**
  list.
- When the person is invited, or signs in through your directory or single sign-on,
  the record becomes a normal account. It keeps its policies and its linked laptop.
- Sign-in, password reset and role changes are refused for a record until it
  becomes an account.

## Notices

When you assign a policy to someone with no laptop linked, a notice says that the
person has no device linked, so the policy does not reach them yet. It links
straight to where you can link a device. Only people who can manage devices see it.

Notices of the same kind are grouped in one card. Select the **X** on a card to
close it. Closed notices move to **Dismissed notices** in **Pending actions**, where
you can find them again.
