Your people can read what their organisation sees through Blindsight. The
**employee notice** is a short, plain statement written from your workspace's real
settings, so it cannot promise something the product does not do. When you change a
setting, the notice changes with it.

## Where people read it

- On the laptop, the **What can my organisation see?** link opens the short form
  in a dialog. It needs desktop app **2.1.13 or later**. Earlier versions show
  their own text, in English.
- A person's exported access record carries the full notice.

The short form lists what your organisation can see (events, protection status,
what happens to text, and how people are named) and what it never sees (details
removed on the laptop, browsing outside AI tools, and sign-in pages). It ends with
*Questions? Contact your administrator.* Rights requests go through your
administrator: Blindsight acts for you and passes a request on to you.

## Languages

The notice is available in **English**, **German**, **French**, **Spanish** and
**Portuguese**. Choose the workspace language at **Settings → Privacy → Language of
the notice**. English is the default.

## What it says about where text goes

The notice follows the real switches and says one of four things:

1. **Sent and kept.** Details such as names, email addresses, card numbers and
   identity numbers are removed on the laptop first. The rest of the message or
   file is sent to the Blindsight service and kept, encrypted, where your
   reviewers can read it.
2. **Sent, not kept.** The text is sent to be checked and not kept. Only counts,
   categories, labels, the file's type and size, and the AI service's name are kept.
3. **Your own Inspector.** The text is checked inside your organisation's own
   systems and does not reach the Blindsight service. A policy that sends text to
   your Inspector while the workspace does not is said to go to the Inspector,
   never to Blindsight.
4. **Stays on the laptop.** The text stays on the device. Only counts, categories,
   labels, file names and the AI service's name reach the service, and of a file
   only its type and size are kept.

Where a policy or an AI service differs from the workspace, the notice says so. A
workspace that shows only totals is not told that reviewers read the text. The
notice never claims that file names are never recorded: it says what is kept.

The notice also says whether names are removed on the laptop. A workspace created
now starts with that on. An existing workspace keeps what it chose, and the notice
says plainly when names are not removed.

## For a works council

The works council page can produce a **pack** in German or English with the same
facts. It reads the same switches as the notice.
