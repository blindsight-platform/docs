---
title: "Linking a workspace"
description: "Asking an existing workspace to become a client instead of creating one: the request lifecycle, what it tells the account, and what happens on approval or decline."
---

If a prospective client already runs its own Blindsight workspace, ask
it to become a client rather than creating a new one. **Clients → Link
existing workspace** needs `msp.clients.manage`.

## Sending the request

You provide:

- **The admin's email** you believe the workspace uses. While a
  request is pending, the account is not told whether that address
  administers a workspace; the response looks the same either way. The
  address has to belong to an admin of the workspace.
- **A note** to them, optional.
- **Licence choice**: whether the workspace **keeps its own licence**
  (it keeps paying Blindsight directly, and your account never changes
  or bills it) or **moves to your seat pool** once approved.

<Note>
  This is the MSP's own choice at the time the request is sent. The
  workspace that answers the request approves or declines it as a
  whole; it is not offered a separate control to pick "keep" versus
  "pool" itself when it responds.
</Note>

## Request lifecycle

- **Expires** after 14 days if nobody answers.
- **Resend** re-arms the same 14-day window and re-sends the email.
- **Cancel** withdraws a still-pending request.
- Creating requests is limited to 10 an hour and 30 a day per account,
  shared by every admin in it. Resending has its own limit of the same
  size.
- A request answered (approved, declined, cancelled, or expired)
  cannot be acted on again; send a new one.

## What happens on approval

The workspace's own admin approves from its side (see [For
clients](/msp/for-clients)). Approval names your account as the parent
and records the client's people and licence choices; no seats move. If
the request's licence choice was "moves to your seat pool", the client
no longer keeps its licence sealed off; you then set its seats and
licence from [Clients](/msp/clients) as you would for any client,
within your account's own allowance.

Approval is refused if the workspace already answers to an MSP,
whichever one. Its admins end that relationship first from **Settings →
Your MSP** (see [For clients](/msp/for-clients#ending-the-link)).
Approval is also refused if your account has used all its
client workspaces. Both your account's audit trail and the client's
record the approval, but your account's own record never shows how the
client proved itself; that is the client's own authentication detail,
recorded only on the client's side, along with any refused attempt to
approve.

## Ending the link later

Either side can end a link after it is made. You release the client
from **Clients** (see [Releasing a client](/msp/clients#releasing-a-client)),
and the client ends it from its own settings. Neither deletes anything
in the client, and a pending request for the same workspace is
cancelled when a link ends.

## What happens on decline

The request is marked declined and you are notified. It cannot be
approved after that; send a new request if you want to ask again.
