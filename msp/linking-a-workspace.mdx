---
title: "Linking a workspace"
description: "Asking an existing workspace to become a client instead of creating one: the request lifecycle, what it tells the account, and what happens on approval or decline."
---

If a prospective client already runs its own Blindsight workspace, ask
it to become a client rather than creating a new one. **Clients → Link
existing workspace** needs `msp.clients.manage`.

## Sending the request

You provide:

- **The admin's email** you believe the workspace uses. The account is
  never told whether that address actually administers a workspace —
  the response looks the same either way, before and after the answer.
- **A note** to them, optional.
- **Licence choice**: whether the workspace **keeps its own licence**
  (it keeps paying Blindsight directly, and your account never changes
  or bills it) or **moves to your seat pool** once approved.

<Note>
  This is the MSP's own choice at the time the request is sent. The
  workspace that answers the request approves or declines it as a
  whole; it is not offered a separate control to pick "keep" versus
  "pool" itself when it responds.
</Note>

## Request lifecycle

- **Expires** after 14 days if nobody answers.
- **Resend** re-arms the same 14-day window and re-sends the email, but
  only once every 10 minutes per request, so the address cannot be
  hammered.
- **Cancel** withdraws a still-pending request.
- Creating and resending requests both count against one limit per
  account — 10 an hour, 30 a day — shared by every admin in the
  account, so two admins of the same account do not each get their own
  budget.
- A request answered (approved, declined, or expired) cannot be acted
  on again; send a new one.

## What happens on approval

The workspace's own admin approves from its side (see [For
clients](/msp/for-clients)). Approval does exactly one thing: it names
your account as the workspace's parent. Nothing else moves
automatically — no seats, no licence change. If the request's licence
choice was "moves to your seat pool", the client no longer keeps its
licence sealed off; you then set its seats and licence from
[Clients](/msp/clients) as you would for any client, within your
account's own allowance.

Approval is refused if the workspace already answers to an MSP,
whichever one — it has to end that link first rather than being handed
a silent takeover. Both your account's audit trail and the client's
record the approval.

## What happens on decline

The request is marked declined and you are notified. It cannot be
approved after that; send a new request if you want to ask again.
