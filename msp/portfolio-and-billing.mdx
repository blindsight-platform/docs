Both pages read from the account's portfolio: counts and states rolled
up across every client, cached for 60 seconds. Nothing from a client's
own content — prompt text, file names, its people — ever crosses into
this view. `msp.clients.view` is required for both.

## Overview

**Overview** (`/overview`) leads with a row of figures for the whole
account:

| Figure | Reads |
| --- | --- |
| **Clients protected** | Clients fully protected, of the total, with how many have gaps or nothing deployed. |
| **Devices inspecting** | Inspecting, of enrolled, across every client, with how many are not inspecting or are outdated. |
| **AI services to decide** | Discovered AI services still awaiting a decision, and in how many clients. |
| **Open alerts** | The count below, with how many are critical. |
| **Seats** | Used, of the pool, with what is still free. |

### Needs attention

A queue of everything worth a look, worst first:

| Severity | What it means | Suggested action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Devices stopped inspecting | Open client |
| High | New AI services awaiting a decision | Open client |
| High | The client switched your access off | Contact client |
| Medium | Desktop agent below the current version | Open client |
| Medium | Licence not issued yet | Blindsight issues it |

Within a severity, the oldest issue comes first. **Open client** takes
you straight into opening that client (see [Opening a
client](/msp/opening-a-client)); the other two are not something the
account can resolve itself.

<Note>
  This queue is computed live from the same counts the figures and the
  Clients table already show — there is no separate alert history yet,
  so an issue that clears (a device starts inspecting again) simply
  drops off the list rather than being recorded as resolved.
</Note>

### Clients with anything open

A short list of every client with at least one open item, as a
shortcut into the full Clients table.

## Clients health columns

See [Clients](/msp/clients#the-clients-table) for the full table.
**Protection** is the headline health signal: **Protected** means every
enrolled device is inspecting, **Gaps** means some are not, **Not
deployed** means nothing is enrolled yet, and **Unknown** means your
access to that client is off, so nothing can be read from it at all.

## Billing

**Billing** (`/msp/billing`) shows the seat pool and what each client
used this month:

| Figure | Reads |
| --- | --- |
| **Seat pool** | Seats used, of the pool your licence sets (or simply "given" if the licence sets no pool), with what is free. |
| **Client workspaces** | The total, with how many are on a trial (trials sit outside the pool and are not counted). |
| **Billable this month** | Peak seats across every non-trial client this calendar month. |
| **Renews** | Your account's own plan tier and renewal date. |

<Note>
  "Peak seats this month" is approximated today by the seats each
  client is using right now, not a true running peak — there is no
  usage-history table behind it yet. It is noted here rather than
  hidden.
</Note>

### By client

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Client** | The workspace name. |
| **Seats given** | Blank for a trial client. |
| **Peak used** | This month, per the note above. |
| **Plan** | "Trial" for a trial client, otherwise its plan tier. |
| **Renews** | The client's own term end date. |

A client that chose to keep its own licence at link approval does not
appear in Billing at all: it is never billed by the account. Trial
clients are excluded from the seat and peak-usage totals, though they
are counted in "client workspaces".

**Export as CSV** downloads the same by-client table
(`msp-billing-YYYYMMDD.csv`). Any cell that could open as a spreadsheet
formula is safely quoted first.
