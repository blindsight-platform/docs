---

**Connect Microsoft** sets up three things with one approval by an Entra
administrator: single sign-on for your tenant, your verified domains, and a sync of
the people in the groups you map to Blindsight roles. It uses Blindsight's own
Microsoft application, the same one as
[Sign in with Microsoft](/permissions-and-access/sign-in), with these application
permissions added.

| Permission | Type | Why | What is read |
| ---------- | ---- | --- | ------------ |
| `Organization.Read.All` | Application | Claims your domains as verified, with no DNS step. | Your tenant's ID, name and verified domains. |
| `User.Read.All` | Application | Keeps console accounts in step with Entra: a person disabled or deleted in Entra loses access. | For each person in a mapped group, or who signed in through it: ID, user principal name, email address, display name, department and whether the account is enabled. |
| `GroupMember.Read.All` | Application | Finds the people in the groups you map to Blindsight roles. | The members of the mapped groups. |

- **Only the mapped groups.** People outside the groups you map to roles are never
  given a console account.
- **Read only.** Blindsight writes nothing to your tenant.
- **Bound to one workspace.** After approval, an administrator of the tenant signs
  in once to bind it. A tenant already bound to another workspace is refused.

A Global Administrator or a Privileged Role Administrator approves it. To use your
own identity provider application instead, see
[Single sign-on and directory sync](/permissions-and-access/single-sign-on).
