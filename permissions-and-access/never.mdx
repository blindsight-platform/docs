---
title: "What Blindsight never does"
sidebarTitle: "What Blindsight never does"
description: "What Blindsight never does with any access it is given to your tenant."
---

With any of the access in this section, Blindsight never:

- reads mailboxes, OneDrive or SharePoint files, Teams chats or calendars
- changes anything in your tenant other than the one Intune app and the fix
  policies you approved ([deploy](/permissions-and-access/intune-deploy), [fixes](/permissions-and-access/intune-fixes))
- stores full prompts or responses read from Microsoft 365
