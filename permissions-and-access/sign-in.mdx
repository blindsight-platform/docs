---

Blindsight's own sign-in application, used when a person selects **Sign in
with Microsoft** or **Sign in with Google**.

| Permission | Type | Why | What is read |
| ---------- | ---- | --- | ------------ |
| `openid` | Delegated | Signs the person in. | A sign-in token for that person. |
| `email` | Delegated | Matches the person to their Blindsight account. | Their email address. A Google address must be verified. |
| `profile` | Delegated | Shows who is signed in. | Their name. |

Nothing else is requested. Blindsight cannot read the person's mail, files,
contacts or calendar, and keeps no Microsoft or Google token after sign-in.
