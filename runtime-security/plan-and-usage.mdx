A workspace on the API starts on a 14 day trial. This page explains what
counts as a request, where you see your usage, and how to choose a plan.

## What a request is

A request is a unit of text of up to 4,000 characters. Every call counts
at least one request. A longer text counts as more requests, rounded up.

| Text in the call | Requests |
| ---------------- | -------- |
| Up to 4,000 characters | 1 |
| 4,001 to 8,000 characters | 2 |
| 100,000 characters | 25 |

What counts in each call:

| Call | Counted |
| ---- | ------- |
| `/scan/input` | `text`, every `history` turn and every `untrusted` part. |
| `/scan/output` | `response`. |
| `/scan/batch` | Each item on its own, at least one each, added together. |
| `/scan/file` | The text read from the file. |
| `/scan/tool-call` | The arguments. |
| `/scan/restore` | Nothing. Restoring is free. |

A call that is refused or fails is not counted.

Every scan answer says what it counted, in the header
`Billable-Requests` and in the `usage` field:

```json
"usage": {"billable_requests": 3, "characters": 9120}
```

A call that you repeat with the same `Idempotency-Key` header and the same
body is not counted again. Use the key to make retries safe. See
[Scan API](/runtime-security/scan-api#retries-and-idempotency).

## Where you see usage

Open **Settings > Plan and usage**. It shows your plan, the requests used
so far against your allowance, and a chart of requests per day. The
**Overview** page shows how many requests your workspace has sent.

Calls also report where you stand. When your plan has a daily allowance,
every scan answer carries the headers `X-Blindsight-Usage-Day-Used`,
`X-Blindsight-Usage-Day-Limit`, `X-Blindsight-Usage-Day-State` and
`X-Blindsight-Usage-Day-Resets-At`, so your application can tell your team
before requests slow down.

## Choose or change a plan

Plans are shown by Stripe. On **Settings > Plan and usage**, select
**Manage on Stripe**. There you choose Pay as you go, Starter or Growth,
change your plan, update your card and download invoices. Come back to the
page afterwards: it shows the plan that is now active.

## At the end of the trial or the allowance

Your service keeps running. If you have not chosen a plan when the trial
ends, or you have used your allowance, requests are served at a reduced pace
until you choose a plan or the next period starts. Nothing is deleted: your
apps, keys, settings and history stay as they are.

When requests are paced, the API answers `429` with a `Retry-After` header.
Wait that many seconds and send the same request again. The SDKs do this for
you. Requests that are paced are not counted. See [Errors](/runtime-security/errors).
